Focus areas

Three pillars guiding Intel's sustainability work

RISE Strategy

Under its RISE (Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling) strategy, Intel sets 2030 goals spanning climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction.

Learn more

Net-Zero Commitment

In 2022, Intel pledged to reach net-zero greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and 2) by 2040, building on decades of environmental initiatives across the semiconductor industry.

Learn more

Water & Waste

Intel restores billions of gallons of water each year and upcycles materials to reduce waste, working with local communities to advance a circular economy.

Sustainability, briefly explained

Intel aims for net-zero greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and 2) across its global operations by 2040, and 100% renewable electricity worldwide by 2030.

Through conservation and watershed-restoration projects, Intel returns billions of gallons of fresh water to the communities where it operates.

Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, and Enabling — Intel's framework of 2030 goals for progress on climate, water, waste, and social impact.
